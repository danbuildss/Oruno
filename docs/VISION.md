# Vision

**Intelligence should move freely.**

## The problem

Whoever uses AI has to pay for it: a $20/month subscription, a card, an account. Many people who want to learn, build or vibe code can't afford that, especially outside the US and Europe.

At the same time, people and organisations would happily pay for others: a developer with spare credit, a creator with an audience, a DAO funding builders, a company growing its developer community, a school. They have no simple, safe way to do it. Today they share keys in chats (which leak), buy credits by hand, or give up.

## Mission

Make access to AI programmable, shareable and fundable by anyone, starting with developers who can't afford subscriptions.

## What Oruno is

The layer that lets **one party fund AI that another party uses**, with limits, transparency, and no subscription for the user.

It starts as **credit drops**: fund credit, set a cap per person, share a link, people claim and build. Underneath is an engine (drops, claims, ledger) that can power much more.

## Where it goes

| Stage | What it becomes |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Credit drops | Anyone funds AI credit and shares it by link |
| 2. Drops for organisations | Communities, DAOs, hackathons, companies and schools run drops with reports |
| 3. Infrastructure for apps | Any AI app lets its users spend sponsor-funded AI through our API |
| 4. Programmable funding on Base | Funding pools in USDC with rules, refunds and onchain transparency |
| 5. Open network and agents | One grant works across apps and providers; AI agents get safe, capped budgets |

Each stage needs proof from the one before. None is promised in advance.

## Principles

1. **Free for the person using it.** The funder pays; the claimer never needs a card or subscription.
2. **Caps are sacred.** Nobody can spend more than their share, and a funder can never be charged more than they put in.
3. **Private by default.** Funders see usage and outcomes, never prompts.
4. **Provider-neutral.** Start on Surplus; never depend on one supplier forever.
5. **Open.** Open-source core and open API, so others can build on it.
6. **Base-native.** USDC on Base for funding and transparency, without forcing users to own a wallet.
7. **Real usage before big architecture.** Build each layer only when the previous one is used.
