# Validation plan

**Status: PROPOSED.** How Drop #1 (Phase 7) tests whether Oruno is worth building further.

## Questions

1. **Will people claim and actually use free capped AI credit?** (Claimer demand)
2. **Can we keep abuse under control on a public link?** (Feasibility)
3. **Do people build something with it?** (Value)
4. **Will anyone other than us want to fund a drop?** (Funder demand — the real business question)
