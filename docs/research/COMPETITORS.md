# Competitors

As of 2026-10-30. Based on web research; competitor claims are their own and not independently verified.

## Finding

No product found where **anyone funds AI credit, sets a cap per person, and shares a claim link** that strangers use in their own AI tools. This is a search result, not proof that none exists.

**What this means for positioning:** labs are moving into gifting, so Oruno's edge must be **programmes, not gifts**: one funder → many people, per-person caps, eligibility and anti-farming, reporting, provider-neutral, Base-native funding. Oruno never shares logins or resells personal subscriptions.

## Why the space is open (assessment)

1. Public free-credit links get farmed.
2. Provider terms restrict sharing subscription credit; a reseller-friendly supply source is needed.
3. Giving credit away has no obvious business model on its own.

## Copy risk

OpenRouter (capped keys already) or Bankr (gateway on Surplus already) could add "share credit" as a feature. Oruno's edge must come from focus on this job, anti-abuse, reporting, and Base-native funding.
