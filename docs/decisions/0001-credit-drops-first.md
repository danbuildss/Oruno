# 0001 — Credit drops shared by link are the first product

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-30

**Context.** We explored a corporate sponsorship pilot (one sponsor, 20 named developers, sales first). The owner's real goal is simpler: let people who can't afford AI subscriptions vibe code for free, funded by anyone, shared publicly without meeting each person.

**Decision.** The first product is a credit drop: a funder sets a total and a cap per person, shares one link, and people sign in to claim a personal capped key.

**Consequences.** Anti-abuse becomes a core feature from day one. Organisation sponsorship moves to Phase 3. Drop #1 is self-funded.
