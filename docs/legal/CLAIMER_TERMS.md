## Oruno — Terms for claiming AI credit

By claiming credit from an Oruno drop, you agree to these terms.

### 1. What you get
- A personal key with a fixed amount of AI credit (the **cap**) and an end date, funded by the drop's funder.
- It is free for you. You will never be charged.
- When the cap is used up or the end date passes, the key stops working.

### 2. What the credit is not
- It is permission to use AI services, not money. It has no cash value.
- It cannot be transferred, sold, shared, exchanged or withdrawn.

### 3. Who can claim
- You must be at least 18 years old.
- You may not claim if you live in, or are a resident of, a country or region under comprehensive U.S. sanctions, or if you are on a U.S. restricted-parties list.
- One claim per person per drop. Using several accounts to claim more than once is not allowed.
- You must meet the drop's sign-in requirements honestly.

### 4. Your key
- Your key is for you only. Do not share, publish or sell it.
- Keep it secret. We show it to you once and do not store it.
- We may revoke any key at any time, including for suspected sharing, farming or misuse.

### 5. Acceptable use
You must follow:
- these terms;
- the Surplus Intelligence Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy (https://www.surplusintelligence.ai/terms-of-service, https://www.surplusintelligence.ai/acceptable-use-policy);
- the terms and usage policies of the AI model providers whose models you use.

You must not use the credit to break the law, harm others, create malware, attack services, attempt to bypass caps or rate limits, jailbreak or red-team models without permission, or violate anyone's privacy or intellectual property.

### 6. Privacy
- Oruno does not store your prompts or the AI's responses.
- To serve your requests, your prompts are sent to Surplus Intelligence and to the sellers and AI model providers that process them. Their own privacy terms apply.
- We store your sign-in account details (such as your GitHub or X username, account creation date and public activity counts) and usage numbers (amount spent, number of requests, days active), to run the drop, prevent abuse and report results.
- The funder sees usage numbers and anything you choose to share about what you built. The funder never sees your prompts.

### 7. Not affiliated with model providers
Oruno is independent. It is not provided by, endorsed by or affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or any other AI model provider.

### 8. No warranty
The service and the AI outputs are provided "as is". AI outputs can be wrong. You are responsible for checking them before relying on them. Models, availability and quality can change.

### 9. Responsibility
You are responsible for how you use your key and for what you send to and do with the AI.

### 10. Changes
We may update these terms. The version shown when you claim applies to that claim.
